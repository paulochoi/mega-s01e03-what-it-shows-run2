What each problem is

  • Parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads the month first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • The booking-v3 hint says MM/DD/YYYY. On booking-v3 tenants’ forms, people type the month first, as the hint tells them. The form then reads the date day first. The parser plays no part, so its fix will not close this.
  • Dates are one day off or differ by time zone. These reports describe a date that moves by one day, or a meeting that falls on different days in Lisbon and Toronto. Day and month are not swapped. The desk notes (desk-notes.json) mark them as a one-day move.

How we counted

The export holds rows that overlap between pages, so reports are counted once by id. Causes come from causes.ts, using each report’s tenant (locale and form) and channel.
